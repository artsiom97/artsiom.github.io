About

My path into design started in 2018 and grew through working with complex digital products where clarity really matters. Over the past few years, I've been designing experiences from B2B SaaS platforms and enterprise tools to mobile apps and marketplaces, turning uncertainty into structure.

Born in Minsk and currently based in Warsaw, I bring a multicultural perspective into my work and value clear communication across teams and contexts. I believe good design starts with asking the right questions, listening closely and collaborating openly.

Outside of work, I slow things down by walking with my dog, observing the city through photography, training boxing and spending time in nature. These moments influence how I design: with patience, curiosity and respect for people.
